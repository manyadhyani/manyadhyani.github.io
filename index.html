About

I am a PhD researcher in Electrical Engineering at Ariel University. My research combines experimental reliability testing of SiC MOSFETs with TCAD simulation of vertical GaN devices. I am interested in understanding semiconductor degradation and how it affects device lifetime.

Research

Experimental research

SiC MOSFET reliability

Investigating device degradation through reliability testing, with an interest in power-law kinetics and time-to-failure prediction.

Device simulation

Vertical GaN devices

Using TCAD simulation to study the physics and electrical behaviour of vertical gallium nitride devices.

Related work: bias temperature instability (BTI) and lifetime modelling in silicon GAA nanosheet transistors, and thermal reliability of I/O connections.
